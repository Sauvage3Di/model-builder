1 · Orient to the cut plane

Teeth should point up and the gum side down, sitting on the blue plane. Auto-orient also shows a rotation sphere: drag the red, green or blue ring to tilt the model about X, Y or Z (hold Shift for 5° steps). The model drops back onto the plane when you let go.

Optional: raise the blue plane to remove everything below it, e.g. bulk material under the gums. Leave it at the bottom to keep everything. The plane stays on until you use another step.

2 · Poly trim

Click Add polygon, then left-click points around the area to remove (on the model or out in empty space). Right-click places the last point and deletes everything inside. While you add points, left-drag is switched off so clicks never move the model. Right-drag still rotates and scroll zooms; a right-click without dragging finishes the polygon. Repeat for more areas.

3 · Smooth periphery

Evens out the rough trimmed edge the base is built from and removes stray bits. Use it after trimming, before generating the base.

4 · Base

Trim first, then click Generate base to preview the finished model.

5 · Hollow (honeycomb)

Opens the underside and fills the inside with honeycomb ribs so resin can drain. Turns the base on automatically. Turn the model over to see the honeycomb.

6 · Engrave text

Engraves the text into the outside wall of the base (the base turns on automatically). Press and drag on the base wall to move it: a cyan outline shows where it will go. Right-drag still rotates the view.
